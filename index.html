Deep Dive: Phase 02

The Financial Audit

A dealership makes its most profit in the "F&I" (Finance and Insurance) office. This is where hidden markups, warranties you don't need, and high-interest rate spreads are added.

We secure a written Buyer's Order before you leave your house. We audit every line item. If we see a $500 "Admin Fee" or a $2,000 "Appearance Package," we negotiate it off or we walk. We ensure your monthly payment is based on the actual math, not a dealer's "rounded up" estimate.
